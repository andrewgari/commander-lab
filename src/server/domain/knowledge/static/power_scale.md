## Power scale (1-10)
Bracket is the hard rule; power is a soft guide. Win turn = turn the deck *consistently threatens* a win (or locks the table, for stax/control). Anchor: **current unmodified precon = 4**. Most casual decks land at 4-7. Self-rated 7s are checked down to 5-6 unless they consistently threaten by T7-8.

| Lvl | Meaning | Win turn | Consistency / tutors | Interaction | Example | ~Bracket |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Barely a deck, joke/theme first | rarely, T13+ | no plan, no tutors | ~none | every card has a hat | B1 |
| 2 | Plan ends at casting commander | T13+ | cards on hand, little ramp/draw | a token removal or two | first brew | B1 |
| 3 | Clear theme, rough structure | T12+ | awkward mana, flavor picks | light | older/weak precon, "any dragon" | B1-B2 |
| 4 | **Precon baseline**, deck functions | T10-11 | enough lands/ramp/draw, sparse tutors | some removal | stock modern precon | B2 |
| 5 | Focused casual, cohesive synergy | T9-10 | tutors find value, not combos | reasonable spread | lightly upgraded precon | B2-B3 |
| 6 | Tuned and consistent, redundancy | T8-9 | several draw engines, a few tutors | cheaper flexible answers | well-upgraded precon | B3 |
| 7 | **Casual ceiling** | T7-8 | strong tutors, some fast mana, maybe late compact combo | real interaction + protection | <=3 Game Changers, best-in-slot fair deck | B3 top / B4 |
| 8 | Optimized high power | T5-7 | fast mana, efficient tutors, compact combos | premium cheap interaction | strongest non-cEDH | B4 |
| 9 | cEDH begins | T3-5 | built on tutors + combo redundancy | cheap/free protection | fringe cEDH | B5 |
| 10 | Top cEDH meta | <=T4 | maximal | free counters, stax | Kraum+Tymna | B5 |

Sources: Command Zone #295 (youtube mgGm_74Lc9M); WotC Brackets Beta article; CoolStuffInc 2026 power levels; CoolStuffInc/Johnson 2023; CommanderSalt algorithm PDF + dev TappedOut post; MTGNexus t=114, t=55331; Draftsim edh-power-level; EDHMeta power-scale-guide; Flipside Gaming; McCormick (Medium, dissent only).
Disagreement: precon anchor ranges 2-5 across sources; chose 4. Command Zone turns are slow (7=T10-12) and were shifted earlier for levels 4-6. Turn ranges for 1-8 are a synthesis, not a quote.

Use: Power is a soft guide that serves the owner's plan. The Bracket is the hard rule and always wins over Power.
