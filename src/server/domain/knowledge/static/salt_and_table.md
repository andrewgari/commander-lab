## Salt and table health
- Salt here is EDHREC's community score, 0 to 4, about how much a card annoys opponents. It does not measure power.
- Labels: high = 2.0 or more; medium = 1.5 to under 2.0; low = under 1.5. Use the score in FACTS and quote its source.
- Salt informs a heads-up to the table ("rule 0" conversation). It is never by itself a reason to cut a card.
- A card matters for table health when it pushes the deck past the owner's bracket target (Game Changer count, mass land denial, chained extra turns, early two-card combos). Say which rule it touches.
- Feel-bad patterns to name when FACTS shows them: locks and stax, mass land denial, heavy taxes, theft, hate pieces that shut off a strategy, sudden instant wins, one-sided board resets.
Sources: EDHREC salt (json.edhrec.com card pages, field "salt"); docs/single-card-review-spec.md (salt is a flag).
