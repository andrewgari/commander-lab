01
THESIS
Kaalia drops a host of Angels straight into combat, haste and extra combats make every swing count, and Demons stay on only to tutor and draw.

Angels were already the biggest of Kaalia's three tribes; Theory leans into them and keeps the Demons that draw cards and the Dragon that gives extra combats.

PILLAR 1
Angel Host
A deep bench of Angels Kaalia can cheat in, with Lyra, Righteous Valkyrie and Sublime Archangel making each one bigger.
PILLAR 2
Haste and Extra Combats
Haste and extra combat phases so Kaalia attacks right away and gets several triggers in one turn.
PILLAR 3
Commander Protection
Mother of Runes, Grand Abolisher, Swat and Teferi's Protection keep Kaalia alive through combat.
PILLAR 4
Trigger Doubling and Cheating
Helm, Quicksilver Amulet, Strionic Resonator and Animate Dead give more ways to put fatties into play.
